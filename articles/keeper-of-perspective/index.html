When You Feel Not Enough

The speaker shares his past struggle:

"I used to feel uncomfortable in rooms like this, in front of people. I used to think I was not enough. I did not value self."

💚 "Comparison is the thief of joy"

A quote often attributed to Theodore Roosevelt. When we measure ourselves against others, we rob ourselves of appreciating our own journey. The speaker uses this reminder when feeling of low self-worth: "It reminds me that I am enough and I'm worthy of love."

When Your Ego Grows Too Large

💀 "Memento Mori"

Latin for "Remember that you will die." An ancient Stoic practice of contemplating mortality — not to be morbid, but to stay grounded. Roman generals celebrating triumphs would have a servant whisper this phrase to them, reminding them they were still mortal despite their glory.

The speaker uses this side of the watch to remember:

"I'm human and I am above no one. Always remember where I came from and remember those who support me — that I don't do it alone."

The Balance

What makes this tool powerful is its duality. It addresses both extremes of the human condition:

  • Feeling too small? → You are enough. Stop comparing.
  • Feeling too big? → You are mortal. Stay humble.

Most of us oscillate between these states. The watch serves as an anchor to the middle ground.

⌚ "A keeper of perspective, not time."
