Indigenous Knowledge

🌍 The 13-Moon Calendar

"All indigenous peoples around the world knew the wisdom of the turtle and followed a 13-month calendar. As there are 13 moon cycles in a year and 27 to 29 days per cycle."

Many indigenous cultures, including various Native American nations, have traditions connecting the turtle shell to lunar calendars. The turtle is often called "the keeper of time" in these traditions.

The Observable Facts

🔬 What's Verifiable

  • 13 central scutes: Most (not all) turtle species have 13 scutes in the central and costal areas
  • 28 marginal scutes: Many turtles have approximately this number around the edge
  • Lunar cycles: There are indeed ~13 lunar cycles per year (~27.3 days each)
  • Indigenous traditions: Many cultures do associate turtle shells with calendars and time-keeping

Whether this represents intentional "encoding" or coincidence is a matter of interpretation.
